Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026. DOT Phone Calendar is a personal, single-user utility.

Data and purpose

With the user's authorization, the utility captures visible calendar information on the user's Android device. Screenshots may contain event titles, dates, times, locations, and other visible details. It uploads these screenshots and capture metadata to a private folder in that user's Google Drive so their authorized personal assistant can interpret schedules and changes.

Google Drive permission

The utility requests only the drive.file scope: access to specific files created or used by the app. It does not request access to the entire Drive, Gmail, or contacts. OAuth credentials are stored in private application storage on the user's device. Initial setup may use a temporary local computer connection.

Storage and sharing

Calendar data stays in the user's device storage and private Google Drive. The user's selected personal assistant may read it only as authorized by the user. It is not posted on this website, sold, used for advertising, or shared with other users. Google Drive and the selected assistant provider process data under their respective policies.

Retention and control

Uploaded snapshots remain until the user removes them. The user may stop the utility, delete its local and Drive files, and revoke its Google account access at any time through Google Account connections. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously uploaded files or information already shared with the user's assistant.

Contact

Contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile. Do not include private calendar data, credentials, or tokens in public reports.
